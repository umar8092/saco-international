What we supply

Every order is packed and labelled to your requirements, from a single unit to full container loads measured in metric tons.

Rice

Our core business. We export Pakistani rice under our own brands and to buyer specifications, with a full range of grades, packing, branding and logistics.

  • Super Basmati
  • Sella (parboiled) rice
  • IRRI-6
  • Broken rice
  • Private label packing
  • Bulk and retail bags

Tea

Green tea and black tea, supplied loose or packed.

Spices

Ground spices and ready blends, including salan masala and other traditional mixes.

Fresh fruits

Seasonal Pakistani fruit for export, led by our mangoes.

Dry fruits

Dry fruits from Afghanistan exported through transit to markets worldwide, including Australia and China, with packing, storage and logistics handled for you.

Charcoal

Charcoal for export, packed to your requirements and shipped in bulk or retail packs.

Carpets

Hand-knotted and machine-made carpets and rugs for importers and retailers.

Gems

Precious and semi-precious stones from the region, sourced for trade buyers.

Kitchenware and leather

Plastic, aluminium and steel kitchenware with custom labelling, and leather garments made to order.

How we work with you

Beyond our own products, we source goods from around the world for our clients and negotiate on their behalf. Our consultation fee is agreed with you up front.

  1. Tell us what you need

    Share the product and the quantity, whether that's a single unit or many metric tons.

  2. We find the right supplier

    We check suppliers worldwide and give you a shortlist with our recommendation.

  3. We negotiate the price

    Our team agrees price and terms with the supplier on your behalf.

  4. You receive your goods

    We follow the order through shipping until it reaches you and you're satisfied.

Transit trade through Pakistan

From our base in Pakistan, close to the Torkham border, we move goods by road between Afghanistan, Pakistan and onward markets.

  • Road transport on established drive routes
  • Customs and transit documentation
  • Storage and repacking
  • Onward shipping through Karachi
